Studio

Founded in 2020, DD Interiors designs and builds interiors for homes and workplaces in Kannur and nearby towns.

Abu Thahir started the studio with a simple idea: a well-planned room should be comfortable to live in and built to last. Every project begins with a site visit and a clear quote, and the design is agreed with the client before any work starts.
